# 06_ECOMMERCE

## SAB’A Ecommerce Codex

This directory defines the complete ecommerce architecture and standards for SAB’A.

The ecommerce ecosystem is not treated as a conventional online store.

It is designed as one continuous environment connecting:

**Discovery → Product → Purchase → Payment → Delivery → Ownership → Relationship**

Every document in this directory defines one part of that environment while remaining connected to the others.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to establish a clear and scalable foundation for SAB’A digital commerce.

It defines:

- How products are presented
- How prices are structured
- How customers purchase
- How payments are processed
- How products are delivered
- How returns and exchanges are handled
- How the overall ecommerce experience is governed

The system must remain commercially effective while protecting the identity and standards of SAB’A.

---

# Ecommerce Philosophy

SAB’A ecommerce follows one central principle:

> **Commerce should serve the relationship, not replace it.**

The customer should experience:

**Clarity → Confidence → Desire → Ease → Care**

The technology behind the experience should remain largely invisible.

---

# Governing Principles

All ecommerce decisions should follow these principles:

1. **Experience before transaction**
2. **Clarity before complexity**
3. **Trust before conversion**
4. **Luxury through restraint**
5. **Consistency across every channel**
6. **Technology serves the experience**
7. **Customer relationship continues after purchase**
8. **The system must scale without losing identity**

# Connected Customer Ecosystem

Ecommerce does not operate independently.

It connects to the wider SAB’A customer ecosystem.

The customer relationship may extend across:

**Website → Account → Email → WhatsApp → Social → AI → Loyalty → Purchase → Delivery → Care**

Every channel must remain connected to the same customer and order reality.

---

# Customer Account

The customer account is an important extension of ecommerce.

It may provide access to:

- Personal information
- Addresses
- Orders
- Order tracking
- Returns
- Wishlist
- Loyalty points
- Rewards
- Coupons
- Membership status
- Preferences

The account should become increasingly valuable as the customer relationship develops.

---

# Loyalty

Loyalty should encourage relationship rather than constant discounting.

Possible mechanisms include:

- Points
- Rewards
- Exclusive access
- Early releases
- Complimentary products
- Special offers
- Shipping benefits
- Product-based rewards

Examples may include:

**Purchase 5 products → Receive the 6th**

or

**Reach a defined loyalty threshold → Unlock a reward**

All loyalty rules must be transparent and system-controlled.

---

# Digital Loyalty Card

Where technically and commercially appropriate, SAB’A may provide a digital loyalty card through supported wallet platforms.

Potential integrations include:

- Apple Wallet
- Google Wallet
- Samsung Wallet

The card may display:

- Membership
- Points
- Rewards
- Offers
- Customer recognition

The wallet is an access point.

The loyalty system remains the source of truth.

# Communication Architecture

Ecommerce communication should have clearly defined roles.

### Transactional Email

Used for:

- Receipts
- Order confirmations
- Payment confirmations
- Shipping updates
- Delivery confirmations
- Refunds
- Return updates

### Marketing / Editorial Email

Used for:

- Newsletters
- New products
- Collections
- Stories
- Launches
- Announcements
- Offers

### Customer Care Email

Used for:

- Complaints
- Questions
- Return assistance
- Order issues
- Customer support

These functions should remain operationally distinct.

# WhatsApp

WhatsApp may support:

- Order updates
- Shipping updates
- Customer care
- AI assistance
- General questions
- Approved campaigns

WhatsApp must remain connected to the central ecommerce system.

It must not become a separate source of truth.

# AI Customer Care

SAB’A AI may support customers across the ecommerce journey.

It may assist with:

- Product discovery
- Product questions
- Order information
- Shipping
- Returns
- General policies
- Account assistance

AI must use verified information.

It must never invent:

- Prices
- Availability
- Delivery dates
- Payment status
- Refund status
- Product specifications

Complex or sensitive situations should be escalated to human customer care.

---

- Product discovery
- Storytelling
- Campaigns

# X Competitions

X may periodically be used for direct community engagement.

Possible activities include:

- Competitions
- Challenges
- Limited coupons
- Product campaigns
- Special rewards

- Rules
- Eligibility
- Duration
- Winner selection
- Reward fulfilment

# Cross-System Consistency

The following information must remain consistent across the entire ecosystem:

- Product information
- Price
- Availability
- Promotions
- Coupons
- Order status
- Payment status
- Shipping status
- Return status
- Loyalty status

The customer should never receive contradictory information from different SAB’A channels.

# Source of Truth

Critical ecommerce information must have one authoritative source.

Customer-facing systems should consume verified information rather than maintaining independent commercial logic.

This protects against:

- Incorrect prices
- Incorrect inventory
- Incorrect order status
- Incorrect shipping information
- Incorrect refund information
- Incorrect loyalty balances

# Change Management

Any significant ecommerce change should be evaluated against the relevant documents in this directory.

A change to one system may affect others.

For example:

**New Payment Method**

may affect:

- Checkout
- Customer communication
- Customer care
- Refunds
- Analytics

Similarly:

**New Loyalty Mechanism**

may affect:

- Customer account
- Pricing
- Promotions
- Checkout
- Payments
- Returns

Changes should therefore be considered as part of the ecosystem rather than isolated features.

# Final Standard

Every ecommerce feature must answer five questions:

1. **Does it improve the customer experience?**
2. **Does it strengthen trust?**
3. **Does it integrate with the wider ecosystem?**
4. **Does it meet the SAB’A standard?**
5. **Can it scale without weakening the identity of the house?**

If the answer to these questions is not clear, the feature requires further consideration before implementation.
