# SAB’A CODEX — SECTION 02: PRODUCT

## README.md

### PRODUCT SECTION OVERVIEW

This document establishes the foundational framework for the `02_Product` section within the SAB’A Codex.

As SAB’A transitions from brand identity definition into physical and olfactory expression, this section serves as the central framework for how SAB’A conceptualizes, develops, evaluates, and structures its fragrance products.

The Product section translates the principles established in `01_Brand` into tangible products while ensuring that every fragrance and physical component remains aligned with SAB’A’s core positioning:

**Luxury Minimalism + Saba Heritage + Contemporary Global Positioning**


## 1. PURPOSE

The Product section bridges the gap between SAB’A’s intangible brand identity and its physical execution.

It defines the principles, structures, standards, and development frameworks required to ensure that every product carrying the SAB’A name reflects the intended level of quality, sophistication, cultural depth, and visual restraint.

The objective is not simply to create fragrances, but to establish a coherent product system in which scent, object, material, proportion, and experience work together as one expression of the brand.


## 2. SCOPE & STRUCTURE

The `02_Product` section governs the development, structure, and standards of SAB’A’s physical and olfactory products.

Future documents within this section will include, but are not limited to:

- **Product Philosophy:** Core principles governing product creation, object design, olfactory expression, and tactile presence.
- **Product Architecture:** Structure of the product collection, sizes, tiers, formats, and lifecycle management.
- **Product Categories:** Definition and organization of current and future product categories.
- **Product Standards:** Core requirements that every SAB’A product must meet.
- **Product Development:** Framework for moving from concept and formulation to testing, refinement, and final production.
- **Quality Standards:** Quality expectations, evaluation criteria, testing principles, and consistency requirements.
- **Product Specifications:** Technical requirements for bottles, caps, sprayers, volumes, materials, and other physical components.
- **Product Naming:** Principles governing fragrance and product naming, including cultural relevance, linguistic clarity, and brand alignment.


## 3. GUIDING PRINCIPLES FOR SAB’A PRODUCTS

Every product developed under the SAB’A umbrella must adhere to the following non-negotiable principles:


### Object Over Container

Products must feel like substantial luxury objects rather than standard commercial containers.

Every physical element should contribute to the perception of quality, permanence, and intentional design.


### Olfactory Integrity

Fragrances must translate the cultural depth and sophistication associated with Saba into modern, globally appealing compositions.

Cultural references should be expressed with depth and restraint rather than relying on clichés, stereotypes, or folkloric decoration.


### Tactile & Visual Cohesion

Materials, proportions, weight, geometry, finishes, and tactile details must work together as one unified product experience.

Heavy glass, clean geometry, minimal lines, and premium finishes should communicate quality before the product is even used.


### Restraint & Intentionality

Every design, material, formulation, and production decision must serve a clear purpose.

Unnecessary ornamentation, excessive visual elements, and over-saturation should be avoided.


### Timelessness

Products should be designed to remain relevant beyond short-term trends.

The goal is to establish objects and fragrances that feel contemporary while retaining a sense of permanence and long-term value.


## 4. CURRENT PRODUCT DIRECTION

SAB’A is currently developing a **fragrance-first product strategy**.

The initial direction includes:

- An initial collection of approximately **four conceptual fragrance directions**.
- **50ml** as the primary launch format.
- **100ml** as a planned future format.
- **10ml** as a planned travel and gifting format.

Current fragrance naming directions include:

- **Ma’rib**
- **Hadramawt**
- **Bilqis**

These names represent current conceptual directions and should not be considered officially finalized until formally approved.


## 5. PHYSICAL PRODUCT DIRECTION

The current physical product direction emphasizes:

- Heavy glass
- Clean geometry
- Minimal lines
- Premium cap construction
- Premium atomizer / sprayer
- Refined proportions
- High-quality tactile materials
- A substantial luxury presence

The bottle and its components should function as part of the brand experience rather than as separate packaging elements.
