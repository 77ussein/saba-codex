# 08_MARKETING

## Purpose

This directory defines how SAB’A approaches marketing while protecting the identity, positioning, and long-term value of the house.

Marketing should connect the right audience with the right SAB’A experience at the right time.

---

## Documents

### `Marketing Philosophy.md`
Defines the principles and mindset behind SAB’A marketing.

### `Brand Positioning.md`
Defines how SAB’A is positioned within its market and how it should be perceived.

### `Audience.md`
Defines the audiences SAB’A serves, their needs, behaviours, and relationship with the brand.

### `Marketing Strategy.md`
Defines the strategic approach used to achieve SAB’A's marketing objectives.

### `Campaigns.md`
Defines how SAB’A plans, develops, executes, and evaluates marketing campaigns.

### `Launch Strategy.md`
Defines how new products, collections, and major initiatives are introduced to the market.

### `Marketing Standards.md`
Defines the standards required to keep marketing consistent, accurate, and aligned with SAB’A.

---

## Marketing Ecosystem

Marketing may extend across:

- Website
- Ecommerce
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Social Media
- Influencer and Creator Partnerships
- Campaigns
- Product Launches
- Events
- Customer Experience
- Loyalty

All marketing activity should remain connected to the wider SAB’A Codex.

---

## Core Principle

Marketing should not manufacture value.

It should reveal, communicate, and connect the value that already exists within SAB’A.

**Meaning → Relevance → Connection → Desire → Relationship**
