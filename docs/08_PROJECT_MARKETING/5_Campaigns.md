# Campaigns
## Purpose
SAB’A campaigns translate the brand, products, and ideas into focused moments of communication.
A campaign should create meaningful discovery, strengthen perception, and support a clear business objective without compromising the identity of SAB’A.
## Campaign Principles
1. **Substance over Hype**
2. **Clarity over Noise**
3. **Invitation over Pressure**
4. **Brand over Trend**
5. **Consistency with Variation**
6. **Experience over Promotion**
7. **Truth over Exaggeration**
## Campaign Objectives
Campaign objectives may include:
- Brand awareness
- Product discovery
- Collection introduction
- Product launch
- Qualified traffic
- Conversion
- Repeat purchase
- Customer retention
- Community engagement
- Seasonal relevance
- Cultural or brand storytelling
Each campaign should have a defined primary objective and measurable supporting objectives.
## Campaign Architecture
**Idea**  
→ **Story**  
→ **Visual Expression**  
→ **Content**  
→ **Distribution**  
→ **Experience**  
→ **Measurement**
The central idea should remain recognizable across every relevant channel.
## Campaign Types
- Brand Campaigns
- Product Campaigns
- Launch Campaigns
- Collection Campaigns
- Seasonal Campaigns
- Editorial Campaigns
- Cultural Campaigns
- Partnership Campaigns
- Retention Campaigns
Campaign types may overlap when a single campaign serves multiple purposes.
## Campaign Development
Every significant campaign should define:
- Campaign name
- Objective
- Central idea
- Audience
- Key message
- Product or subject
- Creative direction
- Visual direction
- Content requirements
- Channels
- Timeline
- Budget
- Call to action
- Success measures
The level of documentation should match the campaign’s scale and complexity.
## Creative Direction
Campaign creative should be:
- Distinctive
- Refined
- Relevant
- Intentional
- Visually coherent
- Consistent with SAB’A
- Appropriate to the campaign objective
Creative decisions should support the idea rather than exist for decoration.
## Messaging
Campaign messaging should follow the established SAB’A voice:
- Intelligent
- Refined
- Human
- Clear
- Distinctive
- Meaningful
Avoid:
- Empty luxury language
- Artificial urgency
- Excessive superlatives
- Aggressive sales language
- Unsupported claims
- Manufactured exclusivity
A campaign should create curiosity before asking for action.
## Channel Adaptation
Campaigns may appear across:
- Website
- Ecommerce
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Social Media
- Paid Media
- Creator Partnerships
- PR
- Events
- Packaging
- Customer Communications
The core campaign idea should remain consistent while execution adapts naturally to each channel.
## Campaign Lifecycle
**Research**  
→ **Concept**  
→ **Planning**  
→ **Production**  
→ **Launch**  
→ **Optimization**  
→ **Evaluation**  
→ **Archive**
Campaigns should be reviewed after completion to identify useful insights, results, and learnings.
## Measurement
Campaign performance may be evaluated through:
- Reach
- Qualified traffic
- Engagement
- Conversion
- Customer acquisition cost
- Revenue
- Repeat purchase
- Retention
- Customer lifetime value
- Return on marketing investment
Performance should be interpreted in relation to the campaign objective rather than through isolated metrics.
## Campaign Review
Before publication, significant campaigns should be reviewed for:
- Brand alignment
- Accuracy
- Creative quality
- Message clarity
- Product accuracy
- Channel suitability
- Legal or regulatory requirements
- Customer experience
- Technical readiness
## Core Principle
> A SAB’A campaign should not simply ask for attention. It should give people a reason to enter, explore, and remember the world of SAB’A.
