# Marketing Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the strategic approach SAB’A uses to build awareness, create demand, develop relationships, and support sustainable growth.

Marketing strategy should connect brand direction with measurable business objectives.

---

## Strategic Principle

SAB’A marketing follows:

**Brand → Audience → Value → Experience → Relationship → Growth**

Marketing should strengthen the brand while contributing to commercial performance.

---

## Strategic Objectives

Marketing may focus on:

- Building brand awareness
- Increasing qualified discovery
- Developing product interest
- Supporting launches
- Increasing conversion
- Encouraging repeat purchase
- Building loyalty
- Developing community
- Strengthening long-term brand equity

Objectives should be prioritised rather than pursued simultaneously without focus.

---

## Growth Model

SAB’A growth may be understood through:

**Discover → Explore → Consider → Purchase → Experience → Return → Recommend**

Each stage requires an appropriate experience and communication strategy.

---

## Acquisition

New audiences may be reached through:

- Organic content
- Paid media
- Search
- Social platforms
- Creators
- Partnerships
- PR
- Events
- Referrals

Acquisition should prioritise relevance and quality over volume alone.

---

## Consideration

Marketing should help people understand why SAB’A is worth exploring.

This may involve:

- Product storytelling
- Education
- Reviews
- Fragrance discovery
- Visual content
- Comparisons where appropriate
- Product demonstrations
- Brand storytelling

The objective is to reduce uncertainty and increase meaningful interest.

---

## Conversion

Conversion experiences should be clear, trustworthy, and aligned with the SAB’A brand.

Key elements may include:

- Product clarity
- Transparent pricing
- Strong product information
- Simple checkout
- Appropriate reassurance
- Relevant calls to action

Conversion should never depend on manipulation.

---

## Retention

The relationship continues after purchase.

Retention may be supported through:

- Product education
- Customer care
- Personalised communication
- New product discovery
- Loyalty
- Relevant recommendations
- Exclusive access

The objective is to create reasons to return beyond discounts.

---

## Loyalty

Loyalty should be based on relationship and experience.

Possible mechanisms include:

- Loyalty programmes
- Early access
- Exclusive products
- Personalised experiences
- Recognition
- Community participation

Loyalty should reward genuine engagement rather than simply encourage spending.

---

## Channel Strategy

Channels should have defined roles.

### Website

Primary destination for deeper brand and product understanding.

### Ecommerce

Primary environment for product discovery and purchase.

### Social Media

Used for discovery, storytelling, community, and conversation.

### Email

Used for editorial, relationship, launches, and relevant commercial communication.

### WhatsApp

Used for direct customer communication, discovery, and service.

### Paid Media

Used to amplify relevant messages and reach qualified audiences.

### Creators & Partnerships

Used when the partner genuinely aligns with SAB’A.

---

## Content and Marketing

Content provides the substance.

Marketing provides the distribution and connection.

The relationship is:

**Content → Attention → Understanding → Interest → Action**

Marketing should not create demand for something that lacks substance.

---

## Campaign Strategy

Campaigns should have:

- A clear objective
- Defined audience
- Central idea
- Key message
- Channel plan
- Timeline
- Budget
- Measurement framework

Campaigns should contribute to the wider SAB’A narrative rather than operate as isolated events.

---

## Launch Strategy

Major launches should be coordinated across:

**Awareness → Curiosity → Reveal → Discovery → Conversion → Post-Launch Relationship**

Launch activity should continue beyond the initial release.

---

## Promotional Strategy

Promotions may be used strategically.

However, SAB’A should avoid creating an audience trained to wait for discounts.

Promotional activity should not weaken:

- Brand perception
- Pricing integrity
- Product value
- Customer trust

---

## Data & Measurement

Marketing performance should be measured using relevant indicators such as:

- Reach
- Qualified traffic
- Engagement
- Conversion
- Customer acquisition cost
- Repeat purchase
- Retention
- Customer lifetime value
- Revenue
- Return on marketing investment

Metrics should support decisions rather than become goals in themselves.

---

## Testing & Learning

Marketing should allow controlled experimentation.

Tests may include:

- Creative
- Messaging
- Audience
- Offer
- Channel
- Landing experience
- Timing

Learning should be documented and used to improve future decisions.

---

## Budget Allocation

Marketing budgets should reflect strategic priorities.

Investment may be distributed across:

- Brand building
- Content
- Paid acquisition
- Campaigns
- Creators
- Partnerships
- Retention
- Testing

Budget decisions should consider both short-term performance and long-term brand value.

---

## Competitive Strategy

SAB’A should not automatically follow competitors.

Competitor activity may provide market information, but strategic decisions should remain grounded in:

- SAB’A identity
- Audience needs
- Product strength
- Business objectives
- Long-term positioning

---

## AI and Marketing Strategy

AI may support:

- Analysis
- Forecasting
- Audience segmentation
- Personalisation
- Campaign development
- Content adaptation
- Reporting

AI outputs should inform decisions rather than replace strategic judgement.

---

## Strategic Review

Marketing strategy should be reviewed periodically against:

- Business performance
- Audience behaviour
- Market changes
- Product development
- Brand health
- Customer feedback

Changes should be deliberate rather than reactive.

---

## Core Principle

Marketing strategy exists to create sustainable demand around genuine value.

The objective is not simply:

**More Reach.**

It is:

**More Relevant Reach → More Meaningful Discovery → More Trust → More Relationships → Sustainable Growth**
