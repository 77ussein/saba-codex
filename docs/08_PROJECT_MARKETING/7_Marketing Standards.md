# Marketing Standards
## Purpose
Marketing standards define the minimum requirements for all SAB’A marketing activities.
They ensure that marketing remains aligned with the brand, accurate in its communication, consistent in execution, and appropriate to the customer relationship SAB’A intends to build.
## Core Standards
All SAB’A marketing should follow these principles:
1. **Brand Alignment**
2. **Accuracy**
3. **Clarity**
4. **Relevance**
5. **Consistency**
6. **Quality**
7. **Integrity**
8. **Purpose**
9. **Channel Suitability**
10. **Long-Term Value**
## Brand Alignment
Marketing must reflect the established SAB’A identity across:
- Brand positioning
- Brand voice
- Visual identity
- Product philosophy
- Fragrance philosophy
- Customer experience
- Cultural and strategic principles
Marketing should strengthen the brand rather than create a separate identity for individual campaigns or channels.
## Messaging Standards
SAB’A messaging should be:
- Intelligent
- Refined
- Human
- Clear
- Distinctive
- Meaningful
Avoid:
- Empty luxury language
- Generic premium claims
- Excessive superlatives
- Aggressive selling
- Artificial urgency
- Manufactured exclusivity
- Manipulative language
- Unsupported claims
Communication should create interest through substance rather than pressure.
## Accuracy Standards
Marketing claims must be accurate, supportable, and consistent with approved product information.
Do not invent or exaggerate:
- Ingredients
- Materials
- Origins
- Craft processes
- Historical associations
- Product benefits
- Scientific claims
- Product performance
- Availability
Any factual claim that materially affects customer understanding should be verified before publication.
## Visual Standards
Marketing visuals should maintain consistency with the SAB’A visual system.
Visual content should demonstrate:
- Intentional composition
- Appropriate typography
- Consistent use of color
- Strong product presentation
- Appropriate imagery
- Technical quality
- Channel suitability
Visual elements should support the message and product rather than compete with them.
## Product Representation
Products must be represented accurately across all marketing channels.
Product presentation should maintain consistency in:
- Product name
- Description
- Size
- Materials
- Fragrance information
- Pricing
- Availability
- Packaging
- Product imagery
Any approved change to product information should be reflected across relevant marketing touchpoints.
## Promotional Standards
Promotions may be used when they serve a legitimate commercial or customer purpose.
They should not:
- Devalue the brand
- Create artificial urgency
- Train customers to wait for discounts
- Rely on misleading comparisons
- Obscure important terms
- Create unnecessary complexity
Promotional communication must clearly explain applicable conditions.
## Channel Standards
Marketing should be adapted to the characteristics of each channel.
The same campaign may require different:
- Formats
- Length
- Visual treatment
- Tone
- Calls to action
- Publishing frequency
Channel adaptation should preserve the central idea and brand integrity.
## Customer Communication
Marketing communication should respect the customer's attention, privacy, and relationship with SAB’A.
Communication should be:
- Relevant
- Appropriate
- Timely
- Clear
- Respectful
Frequency should be managed to avoid unnecessary repetition or fatigue.
## Creator and Partnership Standards
Creators, influencers, media, and strategic partners should be selected based on relevance and alignment rather than reach alone.
Partnerships should consider:
- Brand fit
- Audience relevance
- Reputation
- Content quality
- Authenticity
- Commercial suitability
Any required disclosure or regulatory obligation must be followed.
## Campaign Standards
Significant campaigns should have:
- A defined objective
- A clear central idea
- A defined audience
- Approved messaging
- Appropriate creative direction
- Defined channels
- A launch timeline
- Success measures
Campaign execution should remain consistent with the approved strategy.
## Measurement Standards
Marketing performance should be measured using meaningful indicators appropriate to the objective.
Possible measures include:
- Reach
- Qualified traffic
- Engagement
- Conversion
- Customer acquisition cost
- Revenue
- Repeat purchase
- Retention
- Customer lifetime value
- Return on marketing investment
Metrics should inform decisions rather than become objectives by themselves.
## Review and Approval
Significant marketing materials should be reviewed before publication for:
- Brand alignment
- Accuracy
- Visual quality
- Message clarity
- Product accuracy
- Channel suitability
- Legal and regulatory requirements
- Customer experience
- Technical readiness
The level of review should correspond to the importance and risk of the material.
## Continuous Improvement
Marketing standards should evolve as SAB’A gains experience, customer insight, and operational maturity.
Important learnings should be documented and incorporated into future campaigns, content, and marketing decisions.
## Core Principle
> Marketing should be worthy of the house before it is worthy of publication.
