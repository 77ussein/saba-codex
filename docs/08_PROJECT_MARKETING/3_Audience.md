# Audience

## Purpose

This document defines how SAB’A understands, identifies, and communicates with its audience.

The audience is not simply a market segment or a collection of potential buyers.

It is made up of people who may discover, appreciate, experience, and participate in the world of SAB’A.

---

## Audience Philosophy

SAB’A does not attempt to appeal to everyone.

It seeks meaningful relevance with people who appreciate:

- Fragrance
- Design
- Detail
- Quality
- Craft
- Beauty
- Meaning
- Distinctive experiences

Relevance is more important than reach alone.

---

## Audience Relationship

The desired relationship develops through:

**Awareness → Curiosity → Discovery → Connection → Participation → Loyalty**

A person may enter the SAB’A world without immediately becoming a customer.

That relationship should be allowed to develop naturally.

---

## Audience Mindset

The SAB’A audience may value:

- Thoughtfulness over excess
- Quality over quantity
- Character over conformity
- Discovery over instruction
- Meaning over empty status
- Authenticity over exaggerated claims

These are behavioural and attitudinal characteristics, not rigid demographic requirements.

---

## Audience Segments

SAB’A may identify audiences according to:

- Needs
- Interests
- Behaviours
- Purchase intent
- Product preferences
- Engagement
- Relationship with the brand

Segmentation should remain useful and actionable.

It should not create unnecessary complexity.

---

## Audience Stages

### 1. Curious

Has discovered SAB’A but does not yet understand it.

**Goal:** Create curiosity.

### 2. Explorer

Actively explores products, stories, fragrance, or content.

**Goal:** Build understanding.

### 3. Customer

Has experienced or purchased SAB’A.

**Goal:** Deliver an experience worthy of return.

### 4. Returning Customer

Has developed familiarity and preference.

**Goal:** Strengthen the relationship.

### 5. Advocate

Naturally recommends or shares SAB’A.

**Goal:** Support authentic community and advocacy.

---

## Communication Approach

SAB’A should communicate with respect for the audience's intelligence.

Avoid:

- Talking down to people
- Artificial urgency
- Manipulative messaging
- Empty status language
- Excessive promotional pressure

Prefer:

- Invitation
- Discovery
- Relevance
- Education
- Conversation
- Meaning

---

## Personalisation

Where appropriate, communication may respond to:

- Interests
- Previous purchases
- Browsing behaviour
- Product preferences
- Engagement
- Loyalty

Personalisation should feel useful rather than intrusive.

---

## Audience and Luxury

SAB’A should not tell people that they are luxurious, sophisticated, or special.

It should create an experience that allows people to recognise their own connection with the brand.

The relationship is built through:

**Experience → Recognition → Affinity**

not through direct status claims.

---

## Community

A strong SAB’A audience may develop into a community connected by shared appreciation of:

- Fragrance
- Craft
- Design
- Culture
- Beauty
- Discovery

Community should emerge from genuine shared interest rather than artificial belonging.

---

## Audience Feedback

SAB’A should listen to its audience through:

- Customer conversations
- Reviews
- Questions
- Behaviour
- Purchases
- Content engagement
- Customer service
- Community interaction

Feedback should inform decisions without allowing every individual preference to redefine the brand.

---

## Audience Data

Audience information should be collected and used responsibly.

Data should support:

- Better experiences
- Relevant communication
- Personalisation
- Product understanding
- Business decisions

Privacy and legal requirements must always be respected.

---

## Audience and AI

AI may assist in understanding and serving audiences through:

- Personalised recommendations
- Customer assistance
- Behaviour analysis
- Content adaptation
- Discovery

AI must respect approved privacy, legal, and brand standards.

---

## Core Principle

SAB’A does not ask:

> **How do we make everyone want SAB’A?**

It asks:

> **Who will genuinely appreciate what SAB’A is creating, and how can we help them discover it?**

The objective is not maximum attention.

**It is meaningful connection with the right people.**
