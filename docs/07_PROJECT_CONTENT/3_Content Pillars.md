# Content Pillars

## Purpose

Content Pillars define the core subjects SAB’A consistently communicates.

They provide direction without restricting creativity.

Every important piece of content should connect to one or more pillars.

---

## 1. The World of SAB’A

Content that reveals the world, identity, atmosphere, and point of view of SAB’A.

Examples:

- Brand philosophy
- Culture
- Aesthetic
- Values
- Rituals
- The SAB’A way of seeing things

The audience should feel invited into the world of SAB’A rather than being told that they belong to it.

---

## 2. Fragrance & Sensory Discovery

Content that develops understanding and appreciation of fragrance.

Examples:

- Fragrance families
- Notes
- Materials
- Ingredients
- Scent discovery
- Sensory experiences
- Fragrance education

The goal is to make fragrance more understandable, interesting, and memorable.

---

## 3. Product & Craft

Content that reveals what goes into creating SAB’A products.

Examples:

- Product stories
- Materials
- Craftsmanship
- Design
- Development
- Details
- Quality

Products should be presented through their meaning and substance, not simply as objects for sale.

---

## 4. Knowledge & Education

Content that gives the audience useful knowledge.

Examples:

- Fragrance education
- Materials
- Care
- Usage
- Industry knowledge
- Cultural references
- Technical explanations

Education should remain accessible, intelligent, and engaging.

---

## 5. Stories & Editorial

Content created to build meaning beyond direct product communication.

Examples:

- Stories
- Inspirations
- Cultural references
- Places
- People
- Ideas
- Moments
- Editorial perspectives

This pillar gives SAB’A room to communicate as a house with a point of view.

---

## 6. Community & Participation

Content that creates opportunities for people to interact with SAB’A.

Examples:

- Questions
- Discussions
- Competitions
- Challenges
- Community moments
- Exclusive experiences
- Customer participation

Participation should feel natural and rewarding rather than forced.

---

## 7. Newness & Discovery

Content that introduces what is new within the SAB’A world.

Examples:

- New products
- New collections
- New stories
- New experiences
- Limited releases
- Announcements

Newness should feel like an invitation to discover something, not simply an announcement.

---

## 8. Offers & Loyalty

Commercial content that rewards attention and relationship.

Examples:

- Offers
- Coupons
- Loyalty rewards
- Early access
- Member benefits
- Exclusive opportunities

Commercial communication should remain consistent with the character of SAB’A and should never dominate the content ecosystem.

---

## Pillar Relationship

The pillars work together rather than operating independently.

For example:

**Fragrance**

→ Material  
→ Story  
→ Education  
→ Product  
→ Experience  
→ Community

One idea can therefore produce multiple meaningful forms of content.

---

## Audience Role

The audience should not be treated simply as a buyer.

The desired progression is:

**Discover → Explore → Understand → Connect → Participate → Return**

The objective is to build a relationship with the world of SAB’A.

---

## Content Balance

SAB’A should maintain a healthy balance between:

- Brand
- Education
- Product
- Storytelling
- Community
- Discovery
- Commerce

No single pillar should dominate without a clear strategic reason.

---

## Core Principle

Content Pillars are a framework, not a cage.

They provide direction while allowing SAB’A to remain curious, creative, relevant, and human.

Every piece of content should answer one simple question:

> **Does this strengthen the world of SAB’A?**

If the answer is no, it should be reconsidered.
