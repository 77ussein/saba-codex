# Content Standards

## Purpose

This document defines the standards required for SAB’A content before publication.

Content should be accurate, intentional, distinctive, and consistent with the SAB’A Codex.

---

## Core Standards

Every important piece of content should meet the following standards:

- Brand Alignment
- Accuracy
- Clarity
- Quality
- Relevance
- Consistency
- Purpose
- Channel Suitability

---

## 1. Brand Alignment

Content must reflect the established SAB’A identity.

It should remain consistent with:

- Brand Principles
- Brand Voice
- Visual Identity
- Product Standards
- Content Philosophy

Content must not create a separate personality for SAB’A.

---

## 2. Accuracy

Information must be truthful and supported by reliable sources where appropriate.

Claims involving:

- Products
- Materials
- Ingredients
- Fragrance
- Science
- Sustainability
- History
- Regulations
- Certifications

must be verified before publication.

---

## 3. Clarity

Content should be easy to understand.

Avoid:

- Unnecessary complexity
- Confusing language
- Excessive jargon
- Ambiguous claims
- Unexplained terminology

Clarity should never require sacrificing character.

---

## 4. Writing Quality

Written content should be:

- Clear
- Refined
- Intelligent
- Human
- Purposeful

Avoid empty promotional language, exaggerated claims, and repetitive luxury terminology.

---

## 5. Visual Quality

Visual content should meet SAB’A visual standards for:

- Composition
- Lighting
- Typography
- Colour
- Image quality
- Product accuracy
- Brand consistency

Visual execution should feel intentional rather than improvised.

---

## 6. Purpose

Every important piece of content should have a clear reason to exist.

Possible purposes include:

- Educate
- Inspire
- Introduce
- Reveal
- Connect
- Invite participation
- Support discovery
- Support commerce

Content without meaningful purpose should be reconsidered.

---

## 7. Audience Relevance

Content should provide value to its intended audience.

The audience should be treated as participants in the SAB’A world rather than simply as targets for advertising.

Content should invite curiosity rather than demand attention.

---

## 8. Channel Suitability

Content must be adapted to its destination.

The same idea may appear across:

- Website
- Ecommerce
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- TikTok
- X
- Customer Account
- Packaging

However, each channel should use an appropriate format, tone, length, and rhythm.

---

## 9. Commercial Content

Promotional content must remain consistent with SAB’A's character.

Offers, discounts, launches, and competitions should be:

- Clear
- Honest
- Relevant
- Transparent
- Properly communicated

Commercial activity should not overwhelm the wider content ecosystem.

---

## 10. Audience Invitation

Calls to action should encourage discovery rather than pressure.

Prefer language that communicates:

- Discovery
- Exploration
- Participation
- Curiosity

Avoid unnecessary urgency or manipulative messaging.

---

## 11. AI Content

AI may assist with content creation, adaptation, translation, and personalisation.

AI-generated content must be reviewed before publication.

AI must not independently establish:

- Brand truth
- Product claims
- Scientific claims
- Historical facts
- Commercial promises

Approved SAB’A sources remain authoritative.

---

## 12. Review & Approval

Important content should be reviewed according to its level of risk.

### Standard Content

May require normal editorial and brand review.

### Sensitive Content

May require additional verification when involving:

- Product claims
- Scientific information
- Regulatory matters
- Legal information
- Commercial conditions

### Permanent Content

Long-term website, product, or brand content should receive a higher level of review because it may remain part of SAB’A for an extended period.

---

## Final Content Check

Before publication, ask:

1. Is it accurate?
2. Is it clearly SAB’A?
3. Does it have a purpose?
4. Does it provide value?
5. Is it appropriate for the channel?
6. Is the language natural?
7. Are the claims supported?
8. Does it invite rather than pressure?
9. Would we be comfortable keeping it as part of the SAB’A record?

If the answer to any critical question is no, the content should be revised before publication.

---

## Core Principle

> **Content should be worthy of the house before it is worthy of publication.**

Quality is not an additional step.

**Quality is the standard.**
