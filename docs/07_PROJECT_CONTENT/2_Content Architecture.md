# Content Architecture

## Purpose

The SAB’A Content Architecture defines how content is organised, connected, adapted, and distributed across the SAB’A ecosystem.

It transforms the content philosophy into a practical structure while preserving the identity of the house.

---

## Core Model

SAB’A content follows:

**Identity → Idea → Story → Expression → Channel → Experience**

The same idea may appear across multiple channels, but its expression should adapt to each environment.

---

## Content Layers

Content is organised into five connected layers:

### 1. Brand

Content expresses:

- Identity
- Values
- Principles
- Culture
- Worldview

### 2. Product

Content communicates:

- Products
- Materials
- Craft
- Design
- Fragrance
- Product experience

### 3. Story

Content creates meaning through:

- Stories
- Origins
- Inspiration
- Education
- Editorial narratives

### 4. Community

Content invites the audience to participate through:

- Conversation
- Discovery
- Interaction
- Competitions
- Shared experiences

### 5. Commerce

Content may support:

- Product discovery
- Launches
- Offers
- Loyalty
- Purchases

Commerce should remain a natural extension of the relationship, not the purpose of every piece of content.

---

## Audience Relationship

SAB’A should not communicate with the audience as passive consumers.

The desired relationship is:

**Audience → Participant → Community → Part of the World**

Content should create a feeling of belonging without directly commanding the audience to belong.

The language should invite rather than instruct.

Examples of the approach:

- Enter the world of SAB’A
- Discover what lies within
- A different way of experiencing fragrance
- For those who notice the details

The audience should feel that they have discovered something distinctive rather than being told that they are supposed to feel distinctive.

## Content Adaptation

One idea may become multiple pieces of content.

For example:

**Product Story**

→ Website article  
→ Product-page story  
→ Instagram visual  
→ TikTok video  
→ X conversation  
→ Newsletter feature  
→ WhatsApp message

The information remains consistent.

The expression changes.

---

## Content Journey

A content idea may move through:

**Discovery**

↓

**Interest**

↓

**Understanding**

↓

**Desire**

↓

**Participation**

↓

**Relationship**

↓

**Commerce**

Not every piece of content needs to reach commerce.

Some content exists simply to deepen understanding or strengthen the relationship.

---

## Editorial vs Commercial

The architecture must maintain a healthy balance between:

### Editorial Content

Creates:

- Meaning
- Knowledge
- Culture
- Emotion
- Interest

### Commercial Content

Supports:

- Products
- Launches
- Offers
- Purchases

Commercial content should exist within the larger editorial world of SAB’A.

---

## Content Distribution

Content should be distributed according to:

- Purpose
- Audience
- Format
- Channel
- Timing
- Relevance

Not every piece of content belongs everywhere.

Selective distribution is part of the SAB’A approach.

---

## Repetition and Recognition

Important ideas may be repeated when repetition strengthens recognition.

However, repetition should not become duplication.

The same message can be:

**Repeated in meaning → Reinterpreted in expression → Adapted to context**

This creates consistency without creating monotony.

---

## Personalisation

Where appropriate, content may adapt to:

- Customer interests
- Purchase history
- Loyalty status
- Product preferences
- Previous interactions

Personalisation should feel useful and relevant.

It should never feel intrusive.

---

## AI Layer

AI may assist in:

- Content adaptation
- Content discovery
- Personalisation
- Customer education
- Customer communication
- Content organisation

AI operates within the approved SAB’A content system.

It does not become an independent source of brand truth.

---

## Governance

Important content should have:

- Purpose
- Owner
- Audience
- Source
- Channel
- Status
- Approval requirements

Content involving important product, scientific, regulatory, or commercial claims must be verified before publication.

## Core Principle

SAB’A does not build separate content for separate platforms.

It builds **one content world** that can be experienced through different channels.

The audience should recognise the same SAB’A wherever they encounter it.

**One Identity.  
One World.  
Many Expressions.**
