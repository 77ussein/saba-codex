# 07_CONTENT

## Purpose

This directory defines how SAB’A creates, structures, and manages content across all channels.

Content exists to express the identity of SAB’A, communicate its products and ideas, and build a meaningful relationship with its audience.

---

## Documents

### `Content Philosophy.md`
Defines the philosophy, character, and purpose behind SAB’A content.

### `Content Architecture.md`
Defines how content is organised across subjects, formats, audiences, and channels.

### `Content Pillars.md`
Defines the core subjects and themes SAB’A consistently communicates.

### `Visual Content.md`
Defines the standards and direction for photography, video, imagery, and visual storytelling.

### `Written Content.md`
Defines writing principles, language, tone, and written communication across channels.

### `Product Storytelling.md`
Defines how products, materials, craft, and ideas are transformed into meaningful stories.

### `Content Standards.md`
Defines the quality, accuracy, consistency, and approval standards for SAB’A content.

---

## Content Ecosystem

SAB’A content may extend across:

- Website
- Ecommerce
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Instagram
- TikTok
- X
- Customer Account
- AI Customer Care
- Physical and Packaging Experiences

Each channel should adapt content to its environment while preserving the same SAB’A identity.

---

## Supporting Systems

Content may reference:

- `14_SUPPORT/REFERENCES.md` — trusted sources and research
- `14_SUPPORT/GLOSSARY.md` — shared terminology
- `15_TEMPLATES/TEMPLATES.md` — reusable content structures
- `16_ARCHIVE/ARCHIVE_INDEX.md` — historical content and records

Supporting documentation supports the Codex but does not replace the current Source of Truth.

---

## Core Principle

> **Content should strengthen the world of SAB’A, not simply fill a publishing schedule.**

Every important piece of content should be intentional, accurate, distinctive, and consistent with the SAB’A Codex.
