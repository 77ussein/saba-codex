# Written Content

## Purpose

This document defines how SAB’A communicates through written language across all channels.

Written content should express the identity of SAB’A with clarity, intelligence, refinement, and character.

---

## Writing Philosophy

SAB’A writing should feel:

- Intelligent
- Refined
- Confident
- Human
- Clear
- Distinct
- Meaningful

The language should create interest without forcing attention.

---

## The SAB’A Voice

SAB’A speaks with confidence, but never arrogance.

It is:

**Calm → Intelligent → Sensory → Refined → Human**

The voice should feel established without trying to prove that it is.

---

## Invitation, Not Instruction

SAB’A should invite the audience into its world rather than command them to participate.

Prefer:

> Discover a different expression of fragrance.

Instead of:

> Be luxurious.

Prefer:

> Enter a world shaped by detail.

Instead of:

> Become part of our luxury community.

The audience should feel drawn toward the world of SAB’A naturally.

---

## Language Principles

### 1. Say Less, Mean More

Avoid unnecessary words.

Every sentence should contribute something.

### 2. Be Specific

Prefer real details over vague luxury language.

### 3. Be Sensory

Where appropriate, use language that creates:

- Texture
- Atmosphere
- Emotion
- Memory
- Curiosity

### 4. Be Intelligent

Explain without overcomplicating.

### 5. Be Human

Avoid language that sounds automated, corporate, or generic.

---

## Avoid Empty Luxury Language

Avoid excessive use of words such as:

- Premium
- Exclusive
- Ultimate
- Unparalleled
- Unforgettable
- Luxurious

unless the context genuinely supports them.

Luxury should be communicated through the quality of the idea and the experience, not through repeated claims.

---

## Storytelling Language

SAB’A stories should create curiosity.

A strong story may move through:

**Observation → Detail → Meaning → Emotion**

The reader should discover the idea rather than be told what to feel.

---

## Product Writing

Product descriptions should communicate:

- What the product is
- What makes it distinctive
- Materials or ingredients where relevant
- Sensory characteristics
- Function
- Experience

Claims must remain accurate and supported by approved product information.

---

## Educational Writing

Educational content should make complex subjects understandable.

It should:

- Explain clearly
- Avoid unnecessary jargon
- Respect the intelligence of the reader
- Use evidence where required
- Encourage curiosity

The goal is not to sound academic.

The goal is to make the reader understand.

---

## Social Writing

Social writing may be shorter, faster, and more conversational.

However, it should still preserve the SAB’A voice.

Platform adaptation may change:

- Length
- Rhythm
- Structure
- Vocabulary

It should not change the underlying identity.

---

## Email Writing

Email communication should be clear about its purpose.

### Editorial Email

Should feel like an invitation to discover something.

### Marketing Email

Should communicate commercial information without becoming aggressive.

### Transactional Email

Should prioritise clarity, accuracy, and usefulness.

### Customer Care Email

Should be respectful, helpful, and human.

---

## WhatsApp Writing

WhatsApp communication should feel direct and natural.

Messages should be:

- Clear
- Concise
- Helpful
- Conversational

WhatsApp should not feel like an automated advertising feed.

---

## X Writing

X may allow SAB’A to express:

- Ideas
- Observations
- Opinions
- Announcements
- Community conversation
- Competitions

Writing may be more immediate, but should remain intelligent and recognisable.

---

## Calls to Action

Calls to action should invite rather than pressure.

Prefer:

- Discover
- Explore
- Read the story
- Meet the collection
- Learn more
- Enter the world
- See what is new

Avoid aggressive language such as:

- Buy now!
- Don't miss out!
- Act now!
- You need this!
- Become luxurious!

Urgency should only be used when it is genuinely relevant.

---

## Audience Relationship

The audience should feel respected.

SAB’A does not speak down to people or manipulate them into engagement.

The desired feeling is:

**Curiosity → Recognition → Connection → Participation**

The audience should feel that there is something worth discovering.

---

## Arabic and English

When communicating in Arabic or English, the meaning and character of SAB’A should remain consistent.

Translation should not be purely literal when doing so damages:

- Meaning
- Rhythm
- Cultural relevance
- Emotional tone

Each language should feel naturally written while preserving the same SAB’A identity.

---

## AI-Assisted Writing

AI may assist with:

- Drafting
- Editing
- Translation
- Adaptation
- Content variations
- Personalisation

AI output must be reviewed against SAB’A's:

- Brand Voice
- Content Philosophy
- Product truth
- Content Standards

AI should assist the voice, not replace it.

---

## Accuracy

Written content must not invent:

- Product properties
- Ingredients
- Materials
- Historical facts
- Scientific claims
- Certifications
- Sustainability claims
- Commercial promises

Important claims should be traceable to approved sources.

---

## Writing Review

Before publication, important written content should be reviewed for:

- Purpose
- Clarity
- Brand voice
- Accuracy
- Relevance
- Grammar
- Cultural suitability
- Channel suitability

---

## Core Principle

SAB’A should never sound like it is trying too hard to sound luxurious.

The writing should be confident enough to let the idea speak for itself.

**Invite curiosity.  
Create meaning.  
Respect the reader.  
Let the world of SAB’A speak.**
