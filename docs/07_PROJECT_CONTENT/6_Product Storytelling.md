# Product Storytelling

## Purpose

Product storytelling defines how SAB’A communicates the meaning, character, and experience behind its products.

The goal is to create understanding and curiosity, not exaggerated selling.

---

## Story Structure

A product story may connect:

**Idea → Material → Craft → Detail → Experience → Meaning**

Not every product requires every element.

---

## The Product as a Story

A product should communicate more than what it is.

Where relevant, the story may reveal:

- Why it exists
- What inspired it
- How it was developed
- What makes it distinctive
- What the customer may experience

---

## Truth First

Product stories must always remain connected to reality.

SAB’A must not invent:

- Origins
- Ingredients
- Materials
- Craft processes
- Historical associations
- Scientific claims
- Product benefits

Storytelling may elevate perception, but never through false information.

---

## Sensory Storytelling

Where appropriate, product stories should help the audience imagine the experience through:

- Scent
- Texture
- Appearance
- Atmosphere
- Emotion
- Memory

The objective is to make the product understandable before asking the audience to purchase it.

---

## Product and Craft

Craft should be communicated through meaningful details.

Instead of simply claiming that something is "high quality", explain what creates that quality.

Examples may include:

- Material selection
- Construction
- Finishing
- Fragrance development
- Packaging details
- Production decisions

---

## Product and Audience

The audience should be invited to discover the product rather than instructed what to feel.

Prefer:

> Discover the details behind the composition.

Instead of:

> Experience the ultimate luxury.

The story should leave room for personal interpretation.

---

## Product Story Across Channels

A single product story may be adapted into:

- Product page
- Website editorial
- Instagram
- TikTok
- X
- Email
- WhatsApp
- Packaging

The core information remains consistent while the storytelling format changes.

---

## Launch Storytelling

A new product may be introduced through a sequence:

**Curiosity → Reveal → Story → Detail → Experience → Discovery**

The audience does not need to receive everything at once.

---

## Product Education

Storytelling may also educate the audience about:

- Fragrance
- Materials
- Ingredients
- Design
- Craft
- Usage
- Care

Education should strengthen appreciation of the product.

---

## Emotional Connection

A product story may create:

- Curiosity
- Desire
- Recognition
- Connection
- Memory

Emotion should emerge from the product and its story rather than from exaggerated language.

---

## Commercial Balance

Product storytelling may support commerce, but the story should remain valuable even before the purchase decision.

**Story → Understanding → Desire → Trust → Commerce**

The product should earn attention through meaning.

---

## Core Principle

A SAB’A product should never be presented as simply something to buy.

Its story should allow the audience to discover:

**What it is.  
Why it exists.  
What makes it different.  
What it means.  
What it may become to them.**

The audience should not be told that the product is extraordinary.

**The story should give them a reason to discover it for themselves.**
