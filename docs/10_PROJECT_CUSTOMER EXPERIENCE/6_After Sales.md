# After Sales

## Purpose

After Sales defines how SAB’A maintains the customer relationship after purchase through support, issue resolution, returns, exchanges, product guidance, and continued engagement.

The objective is to ensure that the relationship continues beyond the transaction and that post-purchase interactions reinforce trust in SAB’A.

## After-Sales Principles

1. **Relationship over Transaction**
2. **Care over Convenience**
3. **Resolution over Deflection**
4. **Clarity over Complexity**
5. **Trust over Short-Term Gain**
6. **Relevance over Repetition**
7. **Consistency after Purchase**
8. **Learning from Every Meaningful Interaction**

## The Post-Purchase Relationship

The customer relationship may continue through:

**Purchase**  
→ **Receive**  
→ **Experience**  
→ **Support**  
→ **Return**  
→ **Discover Again**  
→ **Recommend**

The objective is not to create unnecessary communication, but to remain useful and relevant.

## Order Support

Customers should have access to appropriate support for matters such as:

- Order status
- Shipping
- Delivery
- Product availability
- Order changes where possible
- Delivery issues
- Damaged shipments

Information should be accurate and consistent with operational records.

## Product Support

After-sales support may include helping customers understand:

- Product use
- Fragrance characteristics
- Care requirements
- Product differences
- Relevant product information

Product guidance should rely on approved and accurate information.

## Returns and Exchanges

Returns and exchanges should follow the approved SAB’A policy.

Communication should clearly explain:

- Eligibility
- Applicable conditions
- Required steps
- Relevant timelines
- Expected resolution

The process should be as clear and straightforward as the applicable requirements allow.

## Damaged or Defective Products

When a customer reports a damaged or defective product, SAB’A should:

1. Understand the issue
2. Verify the relevant information
3. Assess the appropriate resolution
4. Communicate clearly
5. Complete the agreed action
6. Record significant issues where appropriate

Repeated product issues should be reviewed by the relevant function.

## Complaints

Complaints should be treated as opportunities to understand where the customer experience may have failed.

SAB’A should:

- Listen carefully
- Avoid defensiveness
- Establish the facts
- Take responsibility where appropriate
- Provide a fair resolution
- Identify recurring patterns

A complaint should not be considered resolved simply because the conversation has ended.

## Customer Follow-Up

Follow-up may be appropriate when:

- A problem remains unresolved
- Additional information is required
- A replacement is being processed
- A delivery issue is being investigated
- A significant service recovery has occurred

Follow-up should have a clear purpose and should not create unnecessary communication.

## Retention

Retention should be based on continued relevance and value.

Relevant approaches may include:

- Product education
- New product discovery
- Relevant recommendations
- Early access
- Loyalty experiences
- Meaningful brand communication

Retention should not depend primarily on constant promotions or discounts.

## Re-Purchase

SAB’A may support re-purchase by helping customers:

- Discover related products
- Replenish products where appropriate
- Explore different fragrances
- Discover new collections
- Understand product differences

Recommendations should be relevant rather than driven solely by commercial objectives.

## Customer Feedback

Post-purchase feedback may provide insight into:

- Product satisfaction
- Packaging
- Fulfillment
- Delivery
- Service
- Product expectations
- Areas of friction

Relevant feedback should be documented and shared with the appropriate teams.

## Customer Advocacy

Customers may naturally become advocates when their experience creates enough trust and satisfaction to encourage recommendation.

Advocacy may appear through:

- Reviews
- Word of mouth
- Social sharing
- Recommendations
- Repeat engagement
- Community participation

SAB’A should earn advocacy rather than manufacture it.

## Personalization

After-sales communication may use relevant customer context to improve the experience.

Examples include:

- Previous purchases
- Product preferences
- Relevant recommendations
- Previous service interactions

Personalization should remain useful, respectful, and consistent with privacy requirements.

## Customer Data

Customer information used in after-sales activities should be handled according to:

- SAB’A privacy standards
- Applicable laws
- Data protection requirements
- Internal access controls

Only information necessary for the relevant purpose should be used.

## Service Recovery

When SAB’A fails to meet a reasonable customer expectation, an appropriate service recovery may include:

- Acknowledgment
- Explanation
- Correction
- Replacement
- Refund
- Appropriate goodwill gesture

The response should reflect the seriousness of the issue and applicable policies.

## Operational Feedback Loop

After-sales information should connect back to the wider business.

Relevant insights may inform:

- Product Development
- Packaging
- Operations
- Fulfillment
- Marketing
- Website
- Customer Experience
- Quality

The purpose is to prevent recurring problems and improve the overall system.

## Measurement

After-sales performance may be evaluated through:

- Customer satisfaction
- Resolution time
- Repeat purchase
- Retention
- Return rate
- Complaint frequency
- Customer lifetime value
- Customer feedback
- Referral or advocacy

Metrics should be interpreted alongside qualitative feedback.

## Continuous Improvement

After-sales processes should evolve through:

- Customer feedback
- Service records
- Operational data
- Product learning
- Recurring issues
- Changes in customer expectations

Improvements should make the relationship easier and more valuable without introducing unnecessary complexity.

## Core Principle

> The SAB’A relationship does not end when the product is delivered. It continues through the care that follows.
