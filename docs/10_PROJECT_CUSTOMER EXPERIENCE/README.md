# Customer Experience
## Purpose
The Customer Experience section defines how SAB’A creates, delivers, and maintains a consistent customer experience across every meaningful interaction with the brand.
Customer experience should connect the expectations created by SAB’A with the reality of discovering, purchasing, receiving, using, and returning to the brand.
## Role of Customer Experience
Customer Experience translates SAB’A’s brand principles into real interactions.
It supports:
- Discovery
- Consideration
- Purchase
- Product Experience
- Customer Care
- Retention
- Loyalty
- Advocacy
## Customer Experience Architecture
The customer relationship may be understood through:
**Discover**  
→ **Explore**  
→ **Consider**  
→ **Purchase**  
→ **Receive**  
→ **Experience**  
→ **Return**  
→ **Recommend**
Every stage should feel connected rather than functioning as an isolated interaction.
## Experience Principles
SAB’A customer experience should prioritize:
1. **Respect over Pressure**
2. **Clarity over Friction**
3. **Care over Convenience**
4. **Consistency over Improvisation**
5. **Relevance over Repetition**
6. **Humanity over Automation**
7. **Long-Term Relationship over Single Transactions**
## Customer Journey
The customer journey should be considered across all relevant touchpoints, including:
- Marketing
- Website
- Product Discovery
- Ecommerce
- Checkout
- Communication
- Shipping
- Unboxing
- Product Use
- Customer Service
- After-Sales
- Retention
Each touchpoint should support the wider SAB’A experience.
## Service
Customer service should be:
- Clear
- Respectful
- Responsive
- Knowledgeable
- Helpful
- Consistent
Service should solve the customer's need without unnecessary friction.
## Communication
Customer communication should maintain the established SAB’A voice while adapting to the context of the interaction.
Communication should be:
- Human
- Clear
- Relevant
- Timely
- Accurate
## Packaging Experience
Packaging is part of the customer experience rather than only a protective function.
It should connect:
**Product Protection**  
→ **Presentation**  
→ **Discovery**  
→ **Unboxing**  
→ **Product Experience**
Packaging should remain consistent with approved brand and packaging standards.
## After-Sales
The relationship should continue after purchase.
After-sales experience may include:
- Order support
- Product guidance
- Returns
- Exchanges
- Issue resolution
- Product education
- Relevant recommendations
- Future discovery
The objective is not simply to resolve problems but to maintain trust.
## Feedback
Customer feedback should be treated as a source of insight.
Feedback may reveal:
- Product issues
- Service issues
- Operational friction
- Communication gaps
- Customer preferences
- Opportunities for improvement
Relevant feedback should be documented and connected to the appropriate function.
## Experience Consistency
Customer experience should remain coherent across:
- Brand
- Product
- Website
- Ecommerce
- Marketing
- Packaging
- Customer Service
- Fulfillment
- After-Sales
Different channels may have different formats and behaviors, but the underlying experience should remain recognizably SAB’A.
## Measurement
Customer experience may be evaluated through:
- Customer satisfaction
- Customer feedback
- Response time
- Resolution time
- Return rate
- Repeat purchase
- Retention
- Customer lifetime value
- Customer complaints
- Customer referrals
Metrics should be interpreted alongside qualitative customer feedback.
## Continuous Improvement
Customer experience should evolve through:
- Customer feedback
- Operational learning
- Product development
- Service improvement
- Technology
- New customer needs
- Business growth
Improvements should strengthen the relationship without compromising SAB’A’s identity.
## Core Principle
> SAB’A customer experience should make the quality of the house felt through every interaction, not simply stated by the brand.
