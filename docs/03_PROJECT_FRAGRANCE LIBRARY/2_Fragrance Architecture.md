# FRAGRANCE ARCHITECTURE

The SAB’A Fragrance Architecture defines the structural framework used to understand, develop, and organize a SAB’A fragrance.

It establishes how the main elements of a fragrance relate to one another while leaving specific formulas and material quantities to the appropriate development records.

---

## 1. Fragrance Concept

Every fragrance should begin with a clear creative concept.

The concept establishes the intended:

- Character
- Mood
- Atmosphere
- Inspiration
- Emotional direction

The concept should guide development without unnecessarily restricting the creative process.

---

## 2. Olfactive Structure

A fragrance should have a clear olfactive structure that supports its intended character.

The structure may consider:

- Opening
- Heart
- Base
- Transitions
- Overall balance

These elements should work together as one composition rather than as isolated stages.

---

## 3. Fragrance Character

Each fragrance should have a recognizable character.

Character may be shaped by:

- Materials
- Contrast
- Texture
- Intensity
- Balance
- Evolution

The objective is to create a distinct identity within the SAB’A fragrance collection.

---

## 4. Material Relationship

Fragrance materials should be selected and combined according to their contribution to the overall composition.

No material should exist simply because it is individually appealing.

Materials should support:

- The concept
- The structure
- The character
- The balance
- The intended experience

---

## 5. Evolution Over Time

A fragrance should be considered as a changing sensory experience rather than a static scent.

Development should consider how the fragrance evolves from:

**Opening → Development → Drydown**

The transitions should feel intentional and coherent.

---

## 6. Balance and Harmony

The fragrance structure should maintain an appropriate relationship between its elements.

Balance may involve:

- Intensity
- Contrast
- Proportion
- Complexity
- Smoothness
- Longevity

The objective is not maximum complexity, but a coherent and refined composition.

---

## 7. Collection Relationship

Each fragrance should maintain its own identity while belonging to the wider SAB’A fragrance collection.

The collection should provide:

- Individual distinction
- Overall coherence
- Appropriate variety
- Consistent quality

No fragrance should feel disconnected from the SAB’A house.

---

## 8. Core Principle

The SAB’A Fragrance Architecture can be summarized as:

> **A fragrance should be structured as one coherent composition, from concept to drydown.**

The architecture provides the framework for creative development while allowing each fragrance to develop its own distinctive character.

Specific formulas, percentages, materials, and technical records should be documented separately within the appropriate SAB’A Codex records.
