# FRAGRANCE EVALUATION

The SAB’A Fragrance Evaluation framework defines how fragrances are assessed during development and before approval.

Its purpose is to ensure that fragrance decisions are based on consistent, intentional, and documented evaluation.

---

## 1. Evaluation Purpose

Evaluation should determine whether a fragrance:

- Reflects its intended concept.
- Has a clear and distinctive character.
- Maintains olfactive balance.
- Performs as intended.
- Meets SAB’A quality expectations.

---

## 2. Evaluation Criteria

A fragrance may be evaluated across:

- Opening
- Development
- Drydown
- Balance
- Character
- Quality
- Performance
- Overall experience

Not every criterion requires equal weighting for every fragrance.

---

## 3. Evaluation Method

Evaluation should be conducted consistently using appropriate samples, conditions, and documentation.

Observations should distinguish between:

- Objective findings
- Personal impressions
- Required refinements

---

## 4. Development Feedback

Evaluation feedback should identify what is working, what requires improvement, and why.

Feedback should lead to clear development decisions rather than unnecessary changes.

---

## 5. Approval Evaluation

Before approval, the fragrance should demonstrate alignment with:

- Fragrance concept
- SAB’A fragrance philosophy
- Product standards
- Quality expectations
- Intended performance

Final approval should be documented in the appropriate fragrance record.

---

## 6. Core Principle

The SAB’A Fragrance Evaluation principle can be summarized as:

> **Evaluate objectively, refine intentionally, and approve only when the fragrance is ready.**

Evaluation exists to strengthen the fragrance, not to limit creativity.
