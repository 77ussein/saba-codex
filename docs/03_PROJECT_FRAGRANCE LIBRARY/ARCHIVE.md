# FRAGRANCE ARCHIVE

The SAB’A Fragrance Archive preserves discontinued fragrances, previous development versions, experiments, and historical fragrance records.

Its purpose is to protect the creative and development history of SAB’A while keeping archived work separate from current and approved fragrances.

---

## 1. Archive Purpose

The archive may contain:

- Discontinued fragrances
- Rejected concepts
- Previous formula versions
- Development experiments
- Historical fragrance records

---

## 2. Archive Status

Archived material should remain clearly identified as:

- Archived
- Discontinued
- Rejected
- Superseded

Archived material must not be treated as current or approved.

---

## 3. Traceability

Archived records should retain their relevant:

- Identity
- Version
- Date
- Development history
- Reason for archival, when applicable

This allows SAB’A to understand how fragrance development has evolved over time.

---

## 4. Access and Reference

Archived material may be referenced for:

- Future development
- Historical research
- Creative inspiration
- Internal learning

Archived work should not automatically be reused without appropriate review.

---

## 5. Core Principle

The SAB’A Fragrance Archive principle can be summarized as:

> **Preserve the history without confusing it with the present.**

The archive protects SAB’A’s accumulated fragrance knowledge while keeping the active fragrance library clear and controlled.
