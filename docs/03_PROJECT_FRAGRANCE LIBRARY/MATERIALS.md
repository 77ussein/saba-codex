# FRAGRANCE MATERIALS

The SAB’A Fragrance Materials framework defines how fragrance materials are organized, documented, and evaluated within the SAB’A Fragrance Library.

Materials are treated as building elements of a fragrance composition, not as isolated ingredients.

---

## 1. Purpose

The materials library exists to:

- Organize fragrance materials consistently.
- Support fragrance development.
- Provide a reliable internal reference.
- Record relevant material characteristics.
- Support evaluation and formulation decisions.

---

## 2. Material Classification

Materials may be organized according to their primary olfactive or functional role.

Classification may include:

- Natural Materials
- Aroma Molecules
- Extracts
- Resins
- Essential Oils
- Bases and Accords

Additional classifications may be introduced when required.

---

## 3. Material Profile

Each documented material may include relevant information such as:

- Material Name
- Material Type
- Olfactive Character
- Primary Function
- Usage Considerations
- Supplier or Source
- Reference Information

Only relevant information should be recorded.

---

## 4. Material Evaluation

Materials should be evaluated according to their contribution to the fragrance.

Evaluation may consider:

- Odor quality
- Character
- Intensity
- Compatibility
- Stability
- Performance
- Overall contribution

A material should be selected because it serves the composition.

---

## 5. Quality and Traceability

Materials used in SAB’A development should be identifiable and traceable where appropriate.

Relevant records should be maintained for:

- Source
- Batch
- Version
- Evaluation status
- Development use

This supports consistency and controlled development.

---

## 6. Material Selection

Material selection should support:

- Fragrance concept
- Olfactive structure
- Desired character
- Quality
- Performance
- Safety and applicable requirements

Material availability or popularity alone should not determine selection.

---

## 7. Core Principle

The SAB’A Fragrance Materials principle can be summarized as:

> **Every material should have a purpose within the composition.**

The materials library provides the knowledge and reference system required for fragrance development.

Specific formulas, percentages, and final compositions should be documented separately within **Fragrance Records**.
