# APPENDIX

## Purpose

The Appendix provides supporting information that complements the main SAB’A Codex without replacing its core documents.

It exists to preserve useful context, supporting notes, clarifications, and additional information that may assist understanding or implementation.

## Role

The Appendix may contain information that:

- Supports existing Codex documents
- Provides additional context
- Clarifies concepts or decisions
- Records useful supporting information
- Preserves information that does not require a dedicated primary document

Appendix content should remain relevant to the SAB’A system.

## Relationship with the Codex

The Appendix is a supporting layer of the SAB’A Codex.

It should not override:

- Approved principles
- Established standards
- Official decisions
- Product specifications
- Financial records
- Legal requirements
- Operational procedures

Where a conflict exists, the relevant approved primary document should remain the authoritative reference.

## Supporting Information

Appendix material may include:

- Explanatory notes
- Supporting frameworks
- Additional context
- Reference tables
- Supplementary guidance
- Historical or contextual information
- Useful implementation notes

The level of detail should remain appropriate to its supporting purpose.

## Maintenance

Appendix information should be reviewed when the related primary documentation changes.

Outdated, duplicated, or unnecessary information should be removed or updated to maintain clarity and reliability.

## Documentation Principle

Supporting information should remain traceable to its relevant subject, document, decision, or source whenever practical.

The Appendix should help preserve knowledge without creating unnecessary duplication across the Codex.

## Core Principle

> The Appendix should strengthen the SAB’A Codex by preserving useful supporting knowledge while keeping the primary documentation clear, authoritative, and organized.
