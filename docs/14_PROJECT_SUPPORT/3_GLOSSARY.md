# GLOSSARY

## Purpose

The Glossary defines important terms used throughout the SAB’A Codex.

Its purpose is to maintain consistent terminology, reduce ambiguity, and provide a shared reference for understanding the project.

## Glossary Principles

SAB’A terminology should be:

- Clear
- Consistent
- Practical
- Relevant
- Easy to understand
- Used consistently across related documents

Where a term has a specific SAB’A meaning, that meaning should be used consistently throughout the Codex.

## Brand and Governance

**SAB’A**  
The premium fragrance brand and business system developed under the SAB’A project.

**SAB’A Codex**  
The structured internal documentation system that defines SAB’A's principles, standards, processes, and supporting knowledge.

**Brand Identity**  
The visual, verbal, and conceptual elements that define how SAB’A is recognized and expressed.

**Brand Voice**  
The defined communication style used by SAB’A across relevant customer and business touchpoints.

**Governance**  
The principles, structures, and decision processes used to guide SAB’A.

**Standard**  
An approved requirement or expected level of performance within SAB’A.

## Product and Fragrance

**Product**  
A finished item offered by SAB’A to customers.

**Fragrance**  
The formulated scented composition used in a SAB’A fragrance product.

**Formula**  
The defined composition and proportions of materials used to create a fragrance.

**Fragrance Material**  
A natural or synthetic material used in fragrance development.

**Concentration**  
The proportion of fragrance concentrate within the finished product.

**Batch**  
A defined quantity of product produced under specified conditions and associated with a traceable production record.

**Product Specification**  
The approved requirements that define a product's characteristics, components, and quality expectations.

**Quality Control**  
The activities used to verify that materials, processes, and finished products meet defined requirements.

## Packaging

**Packaging**  
The materials and components used to contain, protect, present, and deliver a product.

**Packaging Component**  
An individual element of packaging, such as a bottle, cap, sprayer, box, or label.

**Packaging Specification**  
The approved requirements defining packaging characteristics, materials, dimensions, and quality.

## Operations

**Operations**  
The activities required to plan, procure, produce, store, fulfill, and support SAB’A products.

**Procurement**  
The process of obtaining required materials, products, components, and services.

**Supplier**  
An external party that provides materials, products, components, or services to SAB’A.

**Lead Time**  
The time required between initiating an order or requirement and receiving the required item or service.

**Inventory**  
Products, materials, components, packaging, and other stock held for business use or sale.

**Fulfillment**  
The process of preparing and completing customer orders through dispatch and delivery.

**Traceability**  
The ability to identify and follow the history, movement, or status of a material, product, batch, or transaction.

## E-commerce and Digital

**E-commerce**  
The digital system through which SAB’A presents, sells, and manages products online.

**Product Catalog**  
The structured collection of products and associated information available through a sales channel.

**Checkout**  
The process through which a customer completes a purchase.

**Customer Account**  
A digital account that stores customer-related information and enables applicable account functionality.

**Conversion**  
The completion of a desired customer action, such as purchasing a product.

## Customer Experience

**Customer Experience**  
The complete perception and interaction a customer has with SAB’A across all relevant touchpoints.

**Customer Journey**  
The sequence of interactions a customer may experience from discovery through purchase, delivery, use, and after-sales.

**Customer Service**  
The support provided to customers before, during, and after a purchase.

**After-Sales**  
Customer support and relationship activities that occur after purchase or delivery.

## Marketing and Content

**Marketing**  
Activities used to create awareness, communicate value, attract customers, and support commercial objectives.

**Campaign**  
A coordinated set of marketing activities designed around a defined objective, audience, message, or period.

**Content**  
Written, visual, audiovisual, or other communication created for SAB’A.

**Product Storytelling**  
The communication of a product's identity, character, inspiration, and value through appropriate content.

## Finance

**Revenue**  
Income generated from the sale of SAB’A products or services.

**Cost**  
An economic resource consumed or committed to create, operate, sell, or deliver value.

**Direct Cost**  
A cost that can be directly associated with a specific product or activity.

**Indirect Cost**  
A cost that supports operations but cannot be directly assigned to a specific product or unit without an allocation method.

**Unit Cost**  
The calculated cost associated with producing or preparing one unit of a product.

**Gross Margin**  
The portion of revenue remaining after applicable direct costs are deducted.

**Contribution Margin**  
The amount remaining from sales after variable costs relevant to the contribution calculation are deducted.

**Cash Flow**  
The movement of cash into and out of the business.

**Working Capital**  
The resources required to support the normal operating cycle of the business.

**Landed Cost**  
The total cost required to bring a product or material to its intended destination, including applicable associated costs.

**Break-Even**  
The point at which relevant revenue equals the costs included in the break-even calculation.

## Roadmap and Development

**Roadmap**  
A structured view of the major direction, stages, priorities, and progression of SAB’A.

**Milestone**  
A significant achievement or progress point used to track development.

**Launch**  
The controlled transition from preparation to commercial availability.

**Future Development**  
Planned or potential development of SAB’A products, capabilities, markets, systems, or experiences beyond the current stage.

**Readiness**  
The degree to which the required conditions for a defined activity have been sufficiently satisfied.

## Documentation

**Codex Document**  
An approved document forming part of the SAB’A Codex.

**Primary Document**  
The authoritative SAB’A document responsible for defining a specific subject or standard.

**Supporting Document**  
A document that provides additional information without replacing the relevant primary document.

**Reference**  
A reliable external or internal source used to support information, decisions, or requirements.

**Appendix**  
Supporting information maintained to complement the primary Codex documents.

**Version**  
A defined revision of a document maintained for controlled documentation.

**Document Status**  
The current approved state of a Codex document.

## Usage

Terms defined in this Glossary should be interpreted according to their SAB’A meaning when used within the Codex.

Where a legal, regulatory, technical, or professional term has a formally defined external meaning, the applicable authoritative definition should take precedence where required.

The Glossary should evolve as SAB’A develops, while unnecessary changes to established terminology should be avoided.

## Core Principle

> SAB’A should use language with the same discipline it applies to its products, operations, and decisions, ensuring that important terms have clear and consistent meaning across the entire Codex.
