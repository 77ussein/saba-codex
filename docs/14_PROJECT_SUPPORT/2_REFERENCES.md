# REFERENCES

## Purpose

REFERENCES provides a structured record of reliable sources that support information, decisions, standards, and important statements within the SAB’A Codex.

Its purpose is to improve credibility, traceability, verification, and future review.

## Reference Principles

SAB’A references should be:

- Reliable
- Relevant
- Verifiable
- Current where applicable
- Clearly identifiable
- Appropriate to the information being supported

Sources should be evaluated according to their authority and relevance to the subject.

## Reference Categories

References may include:

- Official government sources
- Regulatory authorities
- Standards organizations
- Official technical documentation
- Professional industry sources
- Supplier documentation
- Market research
- Academic or scientific sources
- Business and financial references
- Other credible sources

## Official Sources

Official sources should be preferred when information concerns:

- Laws and regulations
- Government requirements
- Product compliance
- Importation
- Taxes and duties
- Business requirements
- Technical standards
- Official platform requirements

## Supplier Sources

Supplier information may be referenced for matters such as:

- Product specifications
- Materials
- Packaging
- Pricing
- Minimum order quantities
- Lead times
- Shipping information

Supplier information should not automatically be treated as independently verified fact.

## Reference Records

Where practical, important references should record:

- Source name
- Organization or author
- Document or page title
- URL or location
- Publication or update date
- Date accessed
- Relevant subject
- Related SAB’A document

## Source Evaluation

Sources should be evaluated before being used for important decisions.

Considerations may include:

- Authority
- Accuracy
- Recency
- Relevance
- Transparency
- Consistency with other reliable sources

Important decisions should not rely on weak or unverified sources when stronger sources are available.

## Traceability

Where a significant claim, requirement, or decision depends on an external source, the relevant reference should be identifiable.

References should allow SAB’A to answer:

- Where did this information come from?
- When was it verified?
- Why was the source considered reliable?
- Which SAB’A document or decision does it support?

## Reference Maintenance

References should be reviewed when:

- Regulations change
- Important standards are updated
- Supplier information changes
- Technical requirements change
- A source becomes unavailable
- New authoritative information becomes available

Outdated references should be updated, replaced, or clearly identified as historical.

## Relationship with the Codex

REFERENCES supports the SAB’A Codex but does not replace its approved documents.

The Codex document remains the primary internal reference for SAB’A decisions and standards.

External references provide supporting evidence where required.

## Core Principle

> SAB’A should know where important information comes from, how reliable it is, and when it was verified.
