## PRIMARY PALETTE


The Primary Palette defines the core colors of the SAB’A brand identity.

These colors represent the brand at its most recognizable across all primary touchpoints and must be used consistently to maintain a cohesive, refined, and timeless visual presence.

As the foundation of the entire visual identity system, the primary colors take precedence over all secondary and accent colors.

This ensures consistency, recognition, and long-term brand integrity across every application.
