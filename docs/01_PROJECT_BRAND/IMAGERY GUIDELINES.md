# IMAGERY GUIDELINES


1. Use simple, clean imagery with soft lighting.

2. Avoid cluttered scenes, harsh filters, or overly styled effects.

3. Highlight craftsmanship, texture, and the bottle’s details.

4. Keep backgrounds neutral, modern, and non-distracting.

5. Aim for a premium, timeless feel that aligns with SAB’A’s luxury positioning.
